# M0 baseline closeout

M0 baseline acquisition, the prescribed reference corpus checks, and pipeline/ABI/device inventory are complete. This closes the baseline and investigation milestone; it does not certify the frozen V13 implementation as a defect-free or regression-free release. Final corrected-source acceptance remains in M1.

## Evidence

- **Criterion:** [combined summary](../../target/backend-parity/m0-existing-continuation-7/combined-summary.json) records 1,522 logical pairs (1,498 numeric and 24 presence), including 630 production pairs, across DX12/Vulkan. The [plan](../../target/backend-parity/m0-existing-continuation-7/plan.json) and [evidence audit](../../target/backend-parity/m0-existing-continuation-7/combined-evidence-audit.json) retain source groups, binaries and units. Acquisition completion does not mean every comparison improved.
- **Resize:** [summary](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-resize-abba-1/summary.json) and [runs](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-resize-abba-1/runs.json) contain eight successful processes, 4,096 production frames and 4,096 separate diagnostic frames. Sixteen route results retain P50/P95/PMax, the actual slowest frame, and update/render-record-submit/wait partitions. This is owned-target resize; it does not measure a window swapchain or prove PMax equals P95.
- **Pipeline/ABI:** [inventory](wgpu-reference-inventory.md) and its [machine-readable form](wgpu-reference-inventory.json) cover 16 WGSL roots, 20 texture-table variants, 179 entrypoint instances and 74 runtime remaps for 37 filters. Preserve [V13 reflection](../../target/backend-parity/m0-shader-inventory-v13/reflection-with-remaps.json), [expanded inputs](../../target/backend-parity/m0-shader-inventory-v13/sources.json), and the original [reflection exporter](../../target/backend-parity/m0-shader-inventory-final/reflect.rs) and [filter remaps exporter](../../target/backend-parity/m0-shader-inventory-final/filter-remaps.rs). These are wgpu reference artifacts, not native HLSL completion.
- **Device/execution:** [SVG route report](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-svg-runtime-1/report.json), [manifest](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-svg-runtime-1/manifest.json), and [pipeline report](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-svg-runtime-1/route-pipelines.json) record four wgpu API/texture routes, capabilities and 41 actual pipelines per route. The historical RTX 4090 LUID is `bf3f010000000000`; the [later boot inventory](../../target/backend-parity/gpu-after-reboot-1/manifest.json) separately records `9f3f010000000000`. Historical identities are not rewritten.
- **Reference corpus:** [final repeat summary](../../target/backend-parity/m0-v13-final-reference-repeats-1/cross-run-summary.json) contains 18 reports and 46,344 identical outputs: SVG/examples/retained, each with three runtime and three precompiled repeats. This covers 1,712 SVGs, 45 examples and 29 retained frames (24 variants). The [human review receipt](../../target/backend-parity/m1-glass-final-1/png-review-approval-1.json) includes the 181 changed M0 images. Earlier failed attempts remain preserved.

## Historical observations retained

The [calibrated scale controls](../../target/backend-parity/m0-scale-calibrated-2/timing-pinned-1/summary.json) completed 12 processes/60 comparisons but did not accept V13 performance: arena5000 was +1.8448% forward and -2.1303% reverse (old/current); arena20000 was +1.5422% forward and noise in reverse. The later [M1 damage correction binding](../../target/backend-parity/m1-damage-demand-1/acceptance-binding-2/summary.json) concerns corrected M1 source, not retroactive acceptance of V13.

V13 also has a documented root-opacity omission: [layer semantics evidence](../../target/backend-parity/m1-layer-semantics-3/pixels-1) records 16 old-version failing states. The [layer-equivalent comparison](../../target/backend-parity/m1-layer-equivalent-2/pixels-1) uses the explicitly corrected layer-domain reference (480 frames/960 RGBA outputs). Reference-corpus equality does not erase this independently discovered defect.

M1 must complete final-source correctness, integration and review before commit/push. The user stopped performance comparisons on 2026-09-13; performance is not accepted or required for this delivery. Native DX12/Vulkan rendering and later hardware coverage retain their own milestones. M2 Metal shader requirements are recorded in the plan and do not expand M0/M1 runtime scope.

Large local benchmark/image artifacts are intentionally referenced in `target/backend-parity`; this document does not imply they are included in a published package.
